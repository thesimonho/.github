# Code of conduct

These rules apply to everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Treat people with respect. Keep feedback focused on the work. Give people room to ask questions and disagree. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are out of bounds.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. Include links or enough context for them to review what happened. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation based on the severity and history of the behavior.
